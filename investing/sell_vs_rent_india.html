Model notes & sources

This tool is inspired by the September 2026 International Real Estate research note. The source example compares a $600,000 home, a $300,000 mortgage at 3.5%, $3,800 monthly rent and a five-year hold.

  • Source article: Sell or Rent Your US Home Before Moving Back to India?
  • IRS: NRA real-property rental income and IRC 871(d)
  • IRS Publication 527: residential rental property and 27.5-year depreciation
  • IRS Publication 523: home-sale exclusion and depreciation after rental use
  • IRS: FIRPTA withholding on foreign sellers

Section 121 eligibility is approximated by the user-entered “years remaining” rather than reconstructed from exact occupancy dates. Depreciation uses a full-year straight-line approximation on the lower of adjusted basis or current FMV, excluding land; the IRS normally applies the mid-month convention.
