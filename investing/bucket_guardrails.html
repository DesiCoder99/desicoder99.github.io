Bucket Guardrails

Three time-segmented buckets + spending guardrails, stress-tested against every rolling start year of real S&P 500 history (1928-2023). All amounts in today's dollars.

Guardrail cuts apply to base spending only, never the mortgage. Bucket 1 is sized as the present value of the first N years of net need; bucket 2 bridges to the SS start age; bucket 3 (100% equities) gets the remainder. Buckets are spent in time order; a depleted bucket falls through to the next. Inflation 2.5% for the nominal-mortgage decay. No taxes or fees. Overlapping historical windows are not independent samples. Monte Carlo modes: block bootstrap resamples blocks of actual history (keeps fat tails and momentum, breaks long-cycle ordering); normal AR(1) draws from a normal distribution with optional year-over-year autocorrelation (thin tails; expected return, volatility and autocorrelation apply to this mode only). The same seeded paths are reused across the corpus solver and the chart sweep.
