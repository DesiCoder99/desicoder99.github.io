1. MVRV regime

Use a rolling 4-year MVRV percentile if available. This explicitly adjusts for cycle compression; a lower percentile means aggregate holder profitability is unusually subdued versus the current regime.

2. Short-term holder stress

STH-MVRV near/below 1 means recent buyers are at breakeven or underwater. That can improve entry quality, provided the broader thesis remains intact.

3. SOPR / realized behavior

SOPR around 1 after loss realization often indicates selling near breakeven. Large sustained values above 1 indicate profit taking rather than distress.

4. Capital absorption

Combines new aggregate cost basis with long-term holder supply trend. Best setup: price stress while realized cap stabilizes/grows and LTH supply rises.

5. Liquidity + ETF flows

Avoids aggressively buying into a deterioration that is still accelerating. Improving liquidity and institutional absorption raise conviction.
