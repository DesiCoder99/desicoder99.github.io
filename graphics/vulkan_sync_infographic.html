INTERACTIVE / TIMELINE SEMAPHORE

One counter. Multiple waiters.

Timeline + Synchronization2

Queue A uploads a buffer. Queue B waits for that upload, then processes it. The CPU waits for a chosen milestone. A timeline wait is satisfied when counter ≥ target; waiting never decrements the counter.
